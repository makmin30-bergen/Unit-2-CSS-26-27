Nova Arena · Patch 7.3

Patch Notes

Season 7 balance update · October 2026

Heroes Maps Bug fixes

Hero changes

This patch slows down the fastest heroes and gives support players more ways to help their team. Every change below was tested in more than 2,000 ranked matches before it shipped.

NovaBlade

Dash cooldown goes from 6 seconds to 8 seconds. NovaBlade could cross the whole map before anyone reacted, and players told us that close games felt decided in the first minute.

PixelFox

Shield now lasts 4 seconds instead of 3, and it can cover two teammates at once. Support players asked for a bigger moment in team fights, and this is it.

Map updates

Glitch City gets a new middle lane with two ways in and one way out. The old lane was too easy to defend, so most matches stalled there for minutes at a time.

“We want every lane to have a reason to fight over it.” — Lead map designer

Bug fixes

  • Fixed a bug where Byte could walk through the north wall.
  • The scoreboard no longer shows a negative number of wins.
  • Sound effects play again after you leave the pause menu.
  • The chat box no longer covers the minimap on small screens.
